Robot: King of the Hill

Wayne Westmoreland & Terry Gilman, 1983 · the original TRS-80 binary, emulated

Controls

ENTER to begin, then 1 or 2 players. Shift and Home together clears the high scores.

About

The same climb, with a robot where the ape used to be.

Published as Smith & Jones Software after our attempt to get Donkey Kong published fell through. Recovered from a cassette image; the assembler left the symbol APEPLAC in the code.

This is not a reimplementation. The original machine code is in the page and a Z80 runs it, so the game, its physics, its scoring and its sound are all the original, at the speed a real Model I ran them. The block graphics are exact; text is drawn from a 5x7 character generator, and choosing Model I also folds lowercase to capitals the way a machine without the lowercase mod did. High scores are kept in this browser.
